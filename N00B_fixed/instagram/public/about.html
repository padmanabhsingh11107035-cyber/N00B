About us

NOOB Socials (also written NOOB or noobsocials) is a social media website and app that students build, run and look after themselves. It lives at noobsocials.com, works in any browser, and can be installed on a phone like a normal app: open the site, then choose “Install” or “Add to Home Screen”.

Everything on NOOB is made to feel like a friendly hang-out rather than a feed to scroll: you share photos, reels and stories, chat with friends, play games together, join live rooms, take on the Daily NOOB challenge, study with an AI helper and even shop from the NOOB store. It is free to join, and you stay in control of who sees what.

Follow us on Instagram: @nooob.xyz.

Want to help? NOOB is run by a student team and new members are welcome: open the app and use the “Join the NOOB team” button, or go to noobsocials.com/?join=team.

Everything you can do on NOOB

📸Share & watch

  • Photo and multi-picture posts with captions, hashtags and music
  • Reels: short vertical videos with sound
  • Longer videos, with playlists
  • Stories that last 24 hours, with polls, questions and link stickers
  • Music Hub: listen to songs and share them in posts and chats

💬Chat & call

  • Private and group chats
  • Voice messages, photos and videos, replies and reactions
  • Voice and video calls
  • End-to-end encrypted chats
  • Translate messages into your language

🎮Play

  • 50+ mini-games: Ludo, Chess, Snakes & Ladders, Monopoly, Tic-Tac-Toe, Rock Paper Scissors and many more
  • Play against smart bots, a friend on the same phone, or someone online
  • Guess the Song, leaderboards and NOOB Points to win

🎙️Go live

  • Live streaming
  • Live Lounge for hosted live sessions
  • NOOB Rooms: voice rooms to talk with friends
  • Meetings you can schedule and join

✨Fun & community

  • Daily NOOB: a new challenge every day, vote for the best entry, top three win NOOB Points
  • Anonymous Messages and Ask Me Anything
  • This or That, Mood Status and public polls
  • Suggestion Box: tell the team what to build next

🧠Create & learn

  • NOOB AI: a friendly AI assistant
  • NOOB Study: an AI study space with plans, practice and notes
  • NOOB Draw: draw and share your own pictures
  • Customer Support with an AI helper

🛍️Shop & rewards

  • Shop NOOB and the NOOB Food Stall
  • NOOB Points you earn by taking part
  • NOOB Pro for extra perks

🔒Privacy & safety

  • Public or private account, close friends, and hiding your profile from chosen people
  • Block and report people, with a team that handles reports
  • Download or delete your data any time
  • Light and dark themes, and many languages

Why students?

NOOB started as a student project and is still built, tested and moderated by students. That is why it keeps changing quickly: new features come from the Suggestion Box, from the team, and from people like you.
