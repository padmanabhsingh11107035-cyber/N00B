About us

NOOB Socials (also written NOOB or noobsocials) is a social media website and app made and run by Padmanabh Singh Vaghela and the NOOB developers. It lives at noobsocials.com, works in any browser, and can be installed on a phone like a normal app: open the site, then choose “Install” or “Add to Home Screen”.

Everything on NOOB is made to feel like a friendly hang-out rather than a feed to scroll: you share photos, reels and stories, chat with friends, play games together, join live rooms, take on the Daily NOOB challenge, talk to NOOB AI and even shop from the NOOB store. It is free to join, and you stay in control of who sees what.

NOOB AI is our free multilingual AI voice assistant with general health information (not medical advice): talk in your own language, hear it answer out loud, and let it remember you. Meet it at ai.noobsocials.com, or sign in with your NOOB account.

Follow NOOB Socials on Instagram: @noobsocials and on YouTube: @noobsocials_456.

Want to help? New members of the NOOB team are welcome: open the app and use the “Join the NOOB team” button, or go to noobsocials.com/?join=team.

Is NOOB safe?

NOOB Socials runs on a secure HTTPS connection with strict security headers, and passwords are never stored in readable form. Our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions say exactly what we do with your data. You can delete your data at any time, and read the full Trust and safety page, and you can send a complaint through our grievance form.

NOOB Socials is made and owned by Padmanabh Singh Vaghela (Founder, CEO and Owner). The only official addresses are noobsocials.com and ai.noobsocials.com.
