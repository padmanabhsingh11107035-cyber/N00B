About NOOB AI

A friendly, multilingual, healthcare-aware AI, made by the NOOB team.

Why we built it

We wanted an AI that talks the way people really talk: in their own language, out loud, and with some care. Most assistants forget you the moment you close them and ignore how you feel. NOOB AI remembers you, notices your mood, and keeps health questions safe, and it is free.

Made by NOOB

NOOB AI is made by Padmanabh Singh Vaghela, the founder, CEO and owner of NOOB Socials (and of NOOB AI), a free social app for reels, videos, chat, 38 games and music. You can sign in to NOOB AI with your NOOB account, and NOOB AI is part of the same family.

What it is made of

  • An app for your browser or home screen (HTML, CSS and JavaScript).
  • A server for speech, AI and memory (Python), with a local database (SQLite).
  • An optional NOOB device with a microphone, speaker and small screen (an ESP32-S3).
