Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about NOOB AI.

What is NOOB AI?

NOOB AI is a free AI voice assistant from the NOOB team. You talk to it in your own language, it answers out loud in the same language, remembers what you tell it and knows a lot about healthcare and everyday topics.

Is NOOB AI free?

Yes. NOOB AI is free to use. Accounts made without a NOOB account get 5 free questions, and linking a NOOB account unlocks unlimited questions.

Which languages does NOOB AI understand?

Every major language, including Hindi, English, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi and Gujarati. It answers in the same language, with a natural voice available in more than 70 languages.

How do I install NOOB AI on my phone?

Open NOOB AI in your phone browser and add it to your home screen. On iPhone use Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android use Chrome, open the menu and tap Install app. See the install guide for details.

Does NOOB AI replace a doctor?

No. NOOB AI gives information and tells you when to see a doctor. In an emergency call 112 or 108. For a mental-health crisis in India call Tele MANAS 14416.

Does NOOB AI remember me?

Yes. It saves what you tell it in its own memory and uses it in later chats. You can search, edit or delete memories on the Memory page.

Who can see my conversations?

Only you. Every person has their own private space on NOOB AI.

How do I sign in?

Choose Continue with NOOB to use your NOOB social account, or create a free account on the sign-in page.

Why is NOOB AI sometimes unavailable?

NOOB AI runs on its own server. If that server is switched off or under maintenance, the app shows a message. Try again a little later.

Can it play songs?

Yes. Ask for a song by name in any language and the app plays it in the YouTube player. Say stop the music to stop it.

Who makes NOOB AI?

NOOB AI is made by the NOOB team, the people behind the NOOB social app at noobsocials.com.
