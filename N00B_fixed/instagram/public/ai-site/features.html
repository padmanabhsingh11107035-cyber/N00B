NOOB AI / Features

Features

A closer look at what NOOB AI can do for you.

Talking

Voice in the app or on a NOOB device

Tap the orb in the app, or hold the button on a NOOB device. You can also type your question.

Conversation mode

The app keeps listening after each answer, so you can chat naturally without tapping again.

Fast answers

The first words come in about two to four seconds, and the voice starts while the rest of the answer is still being written.

Every major language

Speak in Hindi, English, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati and many more. NOOB AI understands you directly from your voice.

Replies in the same language

It answers in the language you spoke, with a natural female voice available in 70+ languages.

Works offline too

If the online brain is unavailable, NOOB AI can fall back to a local AI model on its own computer.

Knowing and remembering

Permanent memory

Everything NOOB AI learns about you is saved in its database and survives a power cut. You can search, edit or delete memories on the Memory page.

About Me

Add your name, age, city, allergies, conditions, medicines and hobbies. NOOB AI uses them in every answer to make advice safer and friendlier.

General knowledge and live search

Science, maths, history, coding, sports, films, law, money, cooking and travel, plus a live web search for anything recent.

Today and now

It knows today's date and time, and can look up news, weather and scores when you ask.

Conversation history

Every question and answer is kept with search, so you can find what NOOB AI told you before.

Plays songs

Ask for a song by name in any language. The app plays it in the YouTube player and you can pause, change or stop it by voice.

Care and safety

Healthcare safety rules

Emergencies come first, with 112 and 108. It explains warning signs instead of guessing a diagnosis and says when a doctor is needed.

Understands your well-being

It notices feelings from your words and tone, responds with warmth, and gently suggests a doctor or counsellor if low mood lasts two weeks or more.

Private mood history

Mood notes are shown only to you, and you can clear them whenever you like.

Read the full safety design

Accounts and devices

Continue with NOOB

Sign in with your NOOB social account, with no new password to remember.

Your own space

Every person has their own memory, chats, About Me and devices. Nobody sees anyone else's data.

Free to try

Accounts made without NOOB get 5 free questions, then link a NOOB account for unlimited use.

Nearby NOOB devices

The app finds NOOB devices on your Wi-Fi and pairs with a 4-digit code on the device screen.

Always in sync

The app shows whether your NOOB device is online, and the device shows whether the computer is on.

Rate your experience

After the first 15 questions NOOB AI asks for a 1 to 5 star rating, so it can keep improving.
