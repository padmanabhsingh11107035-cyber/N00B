Everything a friendly assistant should do

Voice first, private by design, and built to be useful every day.

Talk by voice

Tap the orb and speak. NOOB AI stops listening when you stop talking and answers out loud. Turn on conversation mode to keep chatting like with a friend.

Your language, your voice

It understands every major language, including Hindi, English, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi and Gujarati, and answers in the same language with a natural female voice in 70+ languages.

It remembers you

Tell it your name, your hobbies or your favourite song once. NOOB AI saves it in its own memory and uses it next time, even after it was switched off.

Cares about well-being

It notices how you feel from your words and your tone of voice, answers with warmth first, and checks in when you seem low for days. Your mood history is private.

Knows about almost everything

Science, maths, history, coding, sports, films, cooking, travel. For anything recent, it can look it up on the web: news, weather and scores.

Plays your songs

Say "Play Kesariya". NOOB AI plays it in the YouTube player inside the app, and you can say "stop the music" any time.

Healthcare, with safety first

NOOB AI is an information assistant, not a doctor. It puts emergencies first, points you to 112 or 108 when it matters, shares the Tele MANAS 14416 helpline for mental-health crises, and tells you clearly when you should see a doctor and how soon.
