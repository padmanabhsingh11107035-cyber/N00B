Install NOOB AI

Add NOOB AI to your home screen. It opens full screen, like an app, with its own icon.

iPhone

  1. Open Safari (it must be Safari) and go to ai.noobsocials.com.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow, at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name NOOB AI and tap Add.

Android

  1. Open Chrome and go to ai.noobsocials.com.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm, and the NOOB AI icon appears on your home screen.

Windows and Mac

  1. Open Chrome or Edge and go to ai.noobsocials.com.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the menu and choose Install NOOB AI.

Good to know

The first time, sign in inside the app, because the home-screen app keeps its own storage. Allow the microphone when your phone asks, so voice works. NOOB AI needs an internet connection.
