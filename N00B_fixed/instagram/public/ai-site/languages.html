Speak in your own language

NOOB AI is built for people who think and speak in more than one language.

NOOB AI listens to your voice directly, so you do not choose a language first. Just speak. It works out the language and answers in the same one.

HindiEnglishTamilBengaliMarathiGujaratiand every other major language

What you get

  • It understands every major language, spoken directly.
  • It replies in the language you used.
  • A natural female voice is available in more than 70 languages.
  • You can switch language in the middle of a chat.
  • Songs work in any language: ask for them by name.

The quality of a voice and of recognition can differ from one language to another, and we keep improving it.
