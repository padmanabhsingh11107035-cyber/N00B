Health and safety

NOOB AI is an information assistant, not a doctor. This is how it is built to keep you safe.

In an emergency

Call 112 or 108 now. For a mental-health crisis in India, call Tele MANAS 14416.

What NOOB AI is built to do

  • Emergencies first. For chest pain, breathing trouble, stroke signs, heavy bleeding, poisoning or thoughts of self-harm, it tells you to call 112 or 108 straight away, then gives brief first aid.
  • Help for mental-health crises. It gives the Tele MANAS 14416 helpline.
  • Warmth first. It notices feelings from your words and tone, responds with care, offers one small coping idea, and gently suggests a doctor or counsellor if low mood, panic or sleep trouble lasts two weeks or more.
  • Information, not diagnosis. It explains possibilities and warning signs instead of giving a definite diagnosis.
  • Careful with medicines. It gives only standard label doses for common over-the-counter medicines, with warnings for children, pregnancy, older people and kidney or liver disease. It never advises you to stop a prescribed medicine.
  • Knows you. If you add allergies, conditions, medicines and age to About Me, it uses them to make answers safer.
  • Clear about doctors. It says when you need a doctor and how urgently.

Please remember

NOOB AI can make mistakes. Do not use it as your only source for medical decisions. Always speak to a qualified doctor or pharmacist about your health.
