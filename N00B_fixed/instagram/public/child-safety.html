Child Safety Standards

NOOB Socials (com.noob.social) · noobsocials.com · Last updated 6 November 2026

NOOB Socials is a social app. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualising a minor, sextortion and trafficking. This page explains what we do to keep children and young people safe and how to report a concern.

1. Who can use NOOB

  • NOOB is for people aged 13 and over. Date of birth is asked at sign-up and accounts for people under 13 are refused.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we remove it.

2. What is not allowed

  • Any sexual content involving a child, real or drawn or generated, in posts, reels, stories, chats, profile pictures or anywhere else.
  • Sexual or romantic approaches to a minor, asking a minor for images, or arranging to meet a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Blackmail or threats using a person's images (sextortion).
  • Sharing, selling, asking for or linking to child abuse material.

Accounts that break these rules are suspended or permanently removed.

3. Tools that protect young people

  • Report any account, post, reel, story or chat from inside the app (the three-dots menu). Reports go to the NOOB administrator.
  • Block a person, or hide your profile from them, so they can no longer see or contact you.
  • Private accounts: only approved followers can see the posts.
  • Administrator tools to review reports, remove content and suspend accounts.

4. How we respond

  • Reports about children are treated as the highest priority and reviewed as quickly as we can.
  • Content that breaks these standards is removed and the account is suspended or removed.
  • Where we find child sexual abuse material, we keep the evidence needed and report it to the proper authorities, for example the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and we cooperate with law enforcement requests.

5. Report a concern

Child safety contact: noob.socials@gmail.com

Please write "Child safety" in the subject line and include the user ID (the @name) and a link or description. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first (in India, call 112).

You can also report from inside the app: open the profile or post, tap the three dots and choose Report.

6. Compliance

We follow Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and the laws that apply to us, including child protection laws in India. We review these standards regularly.
