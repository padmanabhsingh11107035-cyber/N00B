Delete Your NOOB Data

You can delete your NOOB account and all of your data yourself, at any time.

It takes less than a minuteDeleting your account removes your data from NOOB straight away. It cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

  • Open nooob.xyz (or the NOOB app) and log in.
  • Go to your Profile, open the ⋮ menu and choose Settings.
  • Open Danger Zone and tap Delete My Account Permanently.
  • Confirm with your password. Your account is deleted immediately.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, posts, reels, stories, comments, messages and uploaded photos, videos and audio. You are also removed from other people’s followers and following lists.

If you signed in with Facebook, Google, Discord or another provider

NOOB only received your name, profile picture and email address from that provider, and uses them to set up your NOOB account. They are deleted together with your account. NOOB never posts to, or reads anything else from, your Facebook or other provider account. You can also remove NOOB from the list of apps connected to your Facebook account in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.

Need help?

If you cannot log in to delete your account, ask NOOB AI Customer Support inside the app and we will delete it for you. More about how we handle your data is in our Privacy Policy.
