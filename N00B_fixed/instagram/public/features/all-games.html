All 37 games on NOOB Socials

NOOB Socials has 37 games: 23 arcade, 6 puzzle, 3 reflex, 3 social, 2 brain. They are free, need no download and run inside the app, on a phone or a computer. Some are big 3D games, some are quick one-minute games, and some are board and party games to play with friends.

Arcade games (23)

  • NOOB Run 3D (Solo): A 3D endless runner: dash through three railway lanes, dodge trains, jump and slide, collect coins and grab power-ups while an inspector and his dog chase you. Missions, skins, weather and a real ranking of every runner best score. Made by NOOB developers.
  • NOOB Party Pack (Solo / Friend): A pack of quick games for 1 to 4 players on one screen: pick how many are playing, pick a game and go. Play against the computer or hand the phone around, and collect trophies for every win. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Strike Bowl (Solo): A five-frame 3D bowling challenge with real pin physics: line up the ball, hold to power up, drag sideways to hook it and knock them all down. 150 is the best possible score. Your best runs are kept on your device. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Lane Runner: Endless (Solo): An endless 3D run-and-gun horde game: lead a squad of real soldiers down a ruined city, steer through gates that add, multiply or arm your squad, dodge eight kinds of traps, melt zombie hordes with automatic fire, pick perks and beat a new boss at the end of every sector. The Daily Run gives everyone the same city. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Frostline Zero (Solo): An avalanche run down a frozen mountain: the avalanche is behind you and the abyss below, so carve, jump and dash through the crystal gallery, dodge the red crystals, reach the checkpoints and ride to the finish as fast as you can. One hit and you are back at the last checkpoint. Your best times are kept on your device. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Farbound: Ruins Beyond (Solo): A small, quiet 3D exploration adventure above the clouds: wander a hill, meet a fox and an old guardian, and uncover ten star memories as the world slowly wakes up. No timers, no enemies, just a story to walk through. Your progress is kept on your device. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Apex Drift (Solo): A 60-second 3D arcade drift score attack: chain controlled slides, build a multiplier and bank as many points as you can before the clock ends. Clip the rings for bonus points, but crash or spin and the chain is lost. A new set of weather, time of day and clip positions every day, and your best runs are kept on your device. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Breachpoint Zero (Solo): A 3D first-person shooter: hold the line through eight waves of Onslaught with four computer squad-mates, use between-wave upgrades, style scoring and Overdrive, and take down the Warden at the end, or play an offline six-bot Deathmatch. Enemy-only auto aim is on by default and your best scores are kept on your device. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Ashenfang: The Colossus Trail (Solo): A single-player 3D action hunt: track three colossal beasts through the Emberwild Basin, read their openings, climb onto their backs and break their weak points before the clock runs out. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Metro Rush (Solo): A colourful 3D endless runner down a three-lane rail line: dodge oncoming trains, jump barriers, roll under signs, surf the train roofs and grab every coin, with a coin magnet, shield, super sneakers and double coins on the way. Pick your style and beat your high score. Made by NOOB developers.
  • TerraFoundry (Solo): Shape a wild, living world: explore a living forest, gather resources, craft tools and build your own shelter while deer, foxes and wolves roam and night brings worse things. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Vertical Platformer (Solo): An endless vertical climber: jump from platform to platform, collect stars, unlock new looks and keep climbing, because there is no finish line. Made by NOOB developers.
  • PulseGrid Arena (Solo): A 3D rhythm-dance game: enter the arrow sequence, then land the beat as the marker crosses the target zone. Chain clean hits, build Fever, climb through the songs from a guided warm-up to the hardest stage, and beware the red arrows: press the opposite direction. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Siegecraft (Solo): Wind back the catapult, find the angle and bring two castles down: a wooden fort and a bluestone keep, built from hundreds of pieces that really fall. Red powder barrels chain the blast, every big hit plays out in slow motion, and the fewer stones you use the more stars you win. Made by NOOB developers. On a phone it plays sideways, and your best scores are kept on your device.
  • Coil Rush (Solo): Steer a glowing cosmic worm, grow by eating energy and swallow planets, stars and comets for permanent powers. Dodge asteroids, energy gates and gravity wells, outgrow the AI worms and take the crown, in free-for-all or 6 v 6 team battles. It works with a mouse, a finger or a gamepad, and your best length is kept on your device.
  • The Rogue: Shadowfall (Solo): A dark-fantasy 3D roguelite. Forge a four-slot, seven-element spell build at the Sigil Forge, choose relics that transform your spells, then Descend through warden-sealed depths or survive the endless Crucible arena. On a phone it plays sideways with two on-screen sticks and buttons.
  • Critter Kart (Solo): Pick one of six critter drivers and race five rivals over three laps of Frosting Loop Valley. Drift for boosts, grab candy gift boxes for items (a bubble shield, a pepper jet, sticky taffy, a homing bee) and use the caramel shortcut. It always plays sideways: on a phone it goes full screen with a steering slider, a brake, a drift and an item button; on a computer use the keyboard or a gamepad.
  • Canyon Rush Circuit (Solo): Drift, boost and jump through a sunlit 3D canyon. Race AI rivals for three laps, or take a friend on in split-screen. Every car is equal, only your line matters. Paint your own car in Car Forge and shape your own circuits in Track Lab. On a phone it plays sideways with an on-screen steering pad.
  • Helix Fall (Solo): Rotate the tower, find the gaps and drop the glowing orb down. Every 90 floors is a level worth 50,000,000 NOOBs, and levels never end. You have one shield per level.
  • Underhall: After Curfew (Solo): A superhero school dungeon in 3D. Fight through two seeded floors under the academy, rescue classmates, pick your powers and take down The Proctor and The Librarian. On a phone it plays sideways with an on-screen joystick and buttons.
  • Neon Alley: Last Call (Solo): A neon pixel-art street brawler. Chain combos, grab street weapons, fight through two stages and take down the rooftop boss.
  • Cyber Snake (1v1 Online): Slither through neon gridlines, collect glowing orbs, and grow your tail.
  • Bubble Pop Blitz (1v1 Online): Aim and pop matching colored bubbles before the ceiling drops.

Puzzle games (6)

  • Royal Ludo (Solo / Friend): The classic race home for 2 to 4 players: roll a six to leave the yard, capture rivals, use the safe star tiles and reach home with an exact roll. Play with friends on one screen or against computer players (relaxed, balanced or strategic), Classic with 4 tokens or Quick with 2. It fits your phone on one screen, with nothing to scroll. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Nocta (Solo / Friend): A premium UNO-style card game for 2 to 10 players: match colour or number, use Skip, Reverse, Draw Two and the wild cards, stack draws, shout NOCTA on your last card and be the first to empty your hand. Play against up to nine computer players or pass the phone around a table. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Color Sort (Solo): Pour the colours from bottle to bottle until each colour fills a bottle of its own: a full bottle gets a cap and is finished. 40 levels, from 3 colours to 14, and every one can be won. Undo as often as you like, and climb the ranking: the highest level cleared by every player.
  • Brick Escape (Solo): A tray full of studded bricks, with doors in its walls. Slide the bricks out of the way and out through the door of their own colour until the tray is empty. 40 levels, from one brick to a packed tray with big bricks and wide doors, and every one can be won. Win up to 3 stars (the fewer moves the better), ask for a hint, undo as often as you like, and climb the ranking: the highest level each player has cleared.
  • Tic Tac Toe Pro (1v1 Online): Challenge a bot or friend in the legendary 3x3 strategic grid battle.
  • Memory Card Match (1v1 Online): Flip and pair the matching cards before time runs out.

Reflex games (3)

  • Flappy Bird 20 (Solo): Tap to flap and fly through the pipes. 20 levels: reach the target score of each level to unlock the next one, and climb the ranking, where your best scores of all levels are added up. Made by NOOB developers.
  • Rock Paper Scissors (1v1 Online): Best of 3 rounds! Test your luck and reaction in the classic hand duel.
  • Reaction Timer Blitz (1v1 Online): Tap immediately when the light turns green. Milliseconds matter!

Social (board and party) games (3)

  • Ludo (Solo / Friend): Roll the dice, race your 4 tokens home, and send opponents back to start. Classic 2-4 player board.
  • Snakes & Ladders (Solo / Friend): Climb ladders, dodge snakes, and race to square 100 first. 2-8 players on one board.
  • Monopoly (Solo / Friend): Buy properties, collect rent, dodge bankruptcy. The classic property trading game, 2-4 players.

Brain games (2)

  • Chess Blitz 3-Min (vs Bot): High-stakes chess vs a genuinely strong bot: win for +50,000,000 NOOBs, but lose vs the bot and your entire balance resets to 0. Play a friend instead for no risk.
  • Speed Math Sprint (1v1 Online): Solve arithmetic equations in 30 high-octane seconds.
