NOOB Games

More than fifty mini games in the noob arena.

What you can do

  • Play alone against a bot or invite a friend to a match with a link.
  • Ludo, Chess, Snakes & Ladders, Monopoly and many more.
  • A leaderboard shows who wins the most.
  • No download needed: games run inside NOOB.
