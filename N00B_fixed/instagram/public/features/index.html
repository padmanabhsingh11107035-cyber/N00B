What you can do on NOOB

NOOB Socials is free and run by students. Pick a part of NOOB to read about it, then log in to open it.

NOOB ReelsShort videos made by NOOB members, one swipe at a time. NOOB VideosLonger videos with a proper player: turn your phone sideways and it fills the screen. NOOB ChatTalk to friends and groups in one place. NOOB ExploreFind new people and new things to watch. NOOB GamesMore than fifty mini games in the noob arena. NOOB MusicA music hub inside your social app. NOOB StoriesMoments that disappear after a day. NOOB PointsBe active, get rewarded.
