NOOB Reels

Short videos made by NOOB members, one swipe at a time.

What you can do

  • Swipe up for the next reel; sound, likes, comments and sharing sit right on the video.
  • Follow the person who made it without leaving the reel.
  • Send a reel straight to a friend from the faces at the top of the page, or share it to WhatsApp, Instagram, X and more.
  • Record or upload your own reel and earn NOOB Points for publishing.

Open NOOB Reels Create a free account

You need a NOOB account to open this page of the app. Log in (or sign up, it is free) and NOOB Reels opens straight away.
