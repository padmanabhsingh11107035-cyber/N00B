NOOB Videos

Longer videos with a proper player: turn your phone sideways and it fills the screen.

What you can do

  • Choose the quality (Auto, 480p, 240p and more) and see roughly how much data each one uses.
  • Mute, change the speed, jump 10 seconds with a double tap, and carry on from where you stopped.
  • Suggested videos wait below the comments in portrait, and beside the video on a computer.
  • A watch history shows every video you started, by date, and you can remove any of them.

Open NOOB Videos Create a free account

You need a NOOB account to open this page of the app. Log in (or sign up, it is free) and NOOB Videos opens straight away.
