How to play

Goal. Be the first to empty your hand. Cards left in other hands score against them; lowest total wins the match.

On your turn. Play a card that matches the top card's colour or number/symbol, or play a wild. If you can't, draw one — if the drawn card is playable you may play it immediately, otherwise your turn ends.

Action cards. Skip jumps the next player. Reverse flips direction (with only two players it acts as Skip). Draw Two forces the next player to draw 2 and lose their turn. Wild lets you choose a colour. Wild Draw Four forces 4 cards plus a colour change.

UNO! When you play your second-to-last card you must hit UNO! immediately. Stay silent and you'll be caught for a 2-card penalty.

Scoring. Number cards = face value. Skip, Reverse, Draw Two = 20. Wild and Wild Draw Four = 50.

House rules. Draw stacking lets you answer a +2 with another +2 to pass the pile on. Jump-in lets anyone holding an identical card play out of turn. Both can be toggled on the start screen.
