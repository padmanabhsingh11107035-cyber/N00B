1. Feed and Stories

The home page has For You and Following tabs, a story tray on top, and posts you can like, comment on, share and save.

  • Posts: photos or several pictures in one post, with a caption, hashtags and music.
  • Stories: a moment that lasts 24 hours, with polls, questions and link stickers. People can reply to it.
  • Highlights: keep your best stories on your profile under a name and a round cover.
  • Notes: a short status note that lasts 24 hours.
  • Alerts: the bell shows new followers, follow requests, likes, comments and shop order updates.

2. Create

The + button makes a post, a reel, a story, an audio track or a note in a few steps, with a look (filter) you can pick. The app sorts what you upload by type: photos go to the Feed and your profile, videos to Reels, audio to the Music Hub.

3. Reels

Full-screen vertical videos with like, comment and share. Swipe up for the next one. Videos you upload as reels appear here automatically.

4. Videos and Go Live

Longer videos in portrait or landscape, with quality choices, suggestions, playlists and a watch history. You can also go live and watch other people’s live streams.

5. Explore

Find people by name or user ID, follow them, and browse posts and reels from across NOOB. A private account must approve you before you can see what it shares.

6. Chat

Private chats and groups, with:

  • voice messages, photos and videos;
  • replies, edits, deleting your own messages, and emoji reactions (hold a message to react);
  • a vanish mode for photos and videos that disappear;
  • shared music tracks, and a mini-game challenge sent inside the chat;
  • translate any message into your language with the globe icon;
  • the emoji picker with every emoji and hundreds of animated stickers and GIFs;
  • the NOOB Global Lounge, a public room for everyone;
  • a colour for your sent messages and a chat background of your choice.

7. End-to-end encrypted chats

Direct chats and private groups are end-to-end encrypted. Text messages, replies, edits, GIFs and stickers are locked on your own device and can only be opened on the devices of the people in that chat. Nobody else can read them, not even NOOB’s servers.

Each chat shows whether it is encrypted, and a security code you can compare with the other person. You can back up your keys with your own passphrase so a new phone can open older messages. Photos, videos, voice notes and shared music are not locked yet.

8. Voice calls

Call people from a chat. Incoming calls ring, and the Calls page next to search in Messages lists your ended, missed and declined calls.

9. Music Hub

Community audio tracks you can listen to and upload (.mp3, .wav). A playing song keeps playing in a small floating player while you use the rest of the app, and tracks can be shared in posts and chats. Guess the Song plays a clip and you pick the title.

10. Games (37 on the Games page)

The Games page is laid out like a game store: the hottest games, people who have played, and rows by category: 24 arcade, 6 puzzle, 3 reflex, 3 social and 2 brain games. 25 of them are bigger games with their own page, including Critter Kart (a kart race with online rooms), Canyon Rush Circuit, Breachpoint Zero, Frostline Zero, Siegecraft, Underhall, Helix Fall, NOOB Run 3D, Color Sort, Brick Escape, Nocta and Royal Ludo.

Games add to the leaderboard and some pay NOOB Points. Play alone, against bots, or challenge a friend from a chat.

11. Wallet and NOOB Points

The Wallet shows your NOOB Points and their history, and your own QR code. Points are earned by taking part and in games, can be sent to friends (pick someone or scan their QR code, then confirm with your password), and can be spent on digital items. Every payment gets its own Payment ID. Scratch cards reveal surprise rewards, and coupons give a percentage off NOOB Pro or verification.

12. NOOB Pro

Five plans paid for with NOOB Points, never with money: Starter, Plus, Pro, Elite and Ultimate. Perks grow with the plan: a Pro badge, ad-free browsing, search priority, profile themes, early access to new games, higher upload limits, a custom accent colour, priority support, an animated profile frame, and the Pro app icons (Pro Black and Pro White). Live Profile Pictures (animated avatars) are a Pro feature too.

13. Verified badge

Profile → ⋮ → Get Verified. Buy a monthly or permanent badge with NOOB Points, or use a verification coupon.

14. Shop NOOB

A real shop for physical products (for example mugs and sticker sheets). Search, sort and filter products, keep a cart, choose pickup or delivery, save up to 10 addresses with an exact map pin, and follow each order from placed to confirmed, ready and completed, with a notification at every step.

15. NOOB Lenses

A camera with 33 face filters in four groups (looks, fun, K-Pop and anime). It works on your device, and a NOOB tag is added to the photo.

16. Buddy Booth

Take a photo with a drawn buddy standing next to you, in a premium frame, then share it. It opens from your profile.

17. NOOB Studio

A photo and video editor for making something look good before you post it.

18. Family Map

Rooms where a family or friends see each other live on a map, only while they choose to share. Places are end-to-end encrypted, a room can be locked, and there is a day report. The room code is the key, so share it only with people you trust.

19. Rooms, Lounge and meetings

NOOB Rooms are live voice rooms to talk with friends. The Live Lounge holds private meeting rooms, and meetings can be scheduled and joined.

20. Daily NOOB and Draw

Daily NOOB gives a new challenge every day: vote for the best entry, and the top three win NOOB Points. NOOB Draw runs the weekly free prize spins.

21. Fun and community

  • This or That: pick a side and see what everyone chose.
  • Ask Me Anything: anonymous questions from friends.
  • Anonymous Messages: messages with no author shown.
  • Mood Status: share how you feel.
  • Public Polls: ask the community.
  • Scratch Reveal: pictures sealed under scratch foil that everybody scratches away.
  • Suggestion Box: tell the team what to build next.

22. NOOB Forms

Build a form or survey and share it by link. People can answer it even without an account, and you see the answers on your own page.

23. NOOB AI and NOOB Study

NOOB AI is a voice assistant you talk to in almost any language. It has its own memory and mood notes that only you can see. NOOB Study is an AI study space with plans, practice and notes. Customer Support has an AI chat and voice helper, a help center and tickets.

24. Accounts, privacy and safety

  • Public, Private and Business accounts. Business accounts get analytics and can apply to run ads.
  • Hide your profile from chosen people, block and restrict people, and report anything. A team handles reports.
  • App lock (Face ID or fingerprint) and login approval: a login on a new device has to be approved from your email.
  • Several accounts on one phone or browser, and a full delete-my-account option.
  • 119 languages, dark and light mode, and 59 settings and activity pages in one hub.
  • On Android: the NOOB app from Google Play, with a choice of app icons.

25. Who makes NOOB

NOOB Socials was founded by Padmanabh Singh Vaghela, its Founder, CEO and Owner, together with a team of student developers. The Our Team page in the app shows the people behind it, and About NOOB tells the story.
