About Padmanabh Singh Vaghela

Padmanabh Singh Vaghela is the founder, CEO and owner of NOOB Socials (also written NOOB or noobsocials), a free social app and website at noobsocials.com, and of NOOB AI, a free multilingual AI voice assistant at ai.noobsocials.com.

NOOB Socials is built for fun: share photos, reels and stories, chat and call friends, play 38 games, listen to music and go live. NOOB AI talks in your own language and answers out loud.
