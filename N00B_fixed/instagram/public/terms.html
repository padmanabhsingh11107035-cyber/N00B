Money, noobs, purchases and refunds

NOOB takes no card, bank or UPI payment inside the app and holds no money for anyone. noobs (NOOB Points) are an in-app game currency: they have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, sold or exchanged for money, and NOOB promises them no value; they are earned by using the app and spent on things inside it, such as NOOB Pro. A NOOB Pro payment gives you a receipt (an "Order Successful" screen with a receipt number); if a payment was taken twice or the plan was not given, send the receipt number through the grievance form within 15 days and the noobs or the plan are put right. The Shop and the Food Stall are run by the sellers who list there: you pay the seller directly (by UPI to the seller's own UPI ID, or cash on delivery or pickup), the address and phone number you give are used only to deliver that order, and refunds and returns are agreed with the seller. NOOB does not sell or deliver goods itself; if a seller does not deliver, report it through the grievance form and NOOB can remove the listing and the seller.

Encrypted chats and translation

Chats are end-to-end encrypted: NOOB's servers keep only locked messages they cannot read. Translate is the one thing that makes a message leave your phone: when you tap Translate, your phone opens that one message and sends only its text to an AI language service (Groq) to be translated. NOOB does not store that text or its translation, but the service sees it for that moment, so a message you translate is not private from that service. If you never tap Translate, nothing is sent.

Young people, live video and location

NOOB is meant for people aged 13 and over; people under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian's knowledge. Family Map, live streaming and chat are optional and off until you turn them on; Family Map shows your place only to the people in the rooms you chose, can be paused or deleted at any time, and is not an emergency or safety service. Report anything unsafe with the Report button or the grievance form: content about a person's private areas, or pretending to be them, is removed within 24 hours of that person's complaint.
