The only official addresses are noobsocials.com and ai.noobsocials.com.

How your data is protected

In transit: the whole site runs over HTTPS, with HSTS and strict browser security headers switched on.

In chats: private chats are end-to-end encrypted between the people in them. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is stored and why.

Where it lives: the website is delivered through Cloudflare and accounts, posts and files are stored with Supabase. Passwords are handled by Supabase sign-in and are never stored in readable form. You can switch on an email approval for logins from a new device, and the app has an App lock.

Backups: the database is backed up every week and uploaded files every month, so a failure does not erase everything.

Checks: automated security tests run before every update goes live. NOOB has not been audited by an independent security company, and we say so in the Terms instead of hiding it.

You stay in control

You can download a copy of your information and delete your account and data yourself: see Delete your data. NOOB does not sell your data.

Reports, moderation and complaints

You can report a post, a person or a message inside the app. For anything else, use the Grievance Officer form (no login needed): you get a ticket number at once, an acknowledgement within 24 hours and an answer within 15 days.

NOOB AI is not a doctor

NOOB AI is an information assistant. It gives general information, not medical advice, and it cannot diagnose or treat anything. For a health problem, speak to a doctor. The AI services behind it are named in the Privacy Policy.

Found a security problem?

Please tell us privately: send it through the Grievance Officer form and say it is a security problem (details in our security.txt). We read every report.
