The house rules

Easy to learn. Hard to leave.

The goal. Race all your tokens around the board and up your own colored home lane. Classic uses four tokens; Quick uses two.

Enter with a six. Roll a six to release a token from its yard onto its colored start tile. A six also earns another roll, even if no move is available.

Make your move. Click or tap a glowing token after rolling. Tokens travel clockwise. You must use the exact number to reach the center; overshooting is not allowed.

Send a rival back. Land on an opponent on an unprotected square to return their token to the yard. All opposing tokens on that unprotected square are captured.

Stay safe. The eight star tiles, including the four colored starts, are safe. Different colors may share them. Your colored home lane is private.

Sharing a square. Tokens can share and pass through squares; this version does not use blockades. Tap a highlighted stack to move its next eligible token. Use keys 1–4 to choose a particular token.

Bonus turns. Capture and home-entry bonuses are optional. A six and bonuses never stack into multiple future turns. If enabled, the third consecutive six forfeits only that roll and ends the turn; earlier moves stay.

Play your way. Add up to four local players or computer opponents. Strategic computers prioritize finishes, captures and safety. Balanced mixes tactics and variety; Relaxed chooses randomly.

Your progress. Games auto-save in this browser when storage is available. Downloaded files may have different storage permissions across browsers. Keep the same HTML file and browser to resume.

Rankings. By default, the first player to bring every token home wins. Turn on “Finish the rankings” to play out the remaining places.
