| `NoobSans-Bold.woff2` | CC0-1.0 | [NoobSans-CC0.txt](NoobSans-CC0.txt) |
| `NoobSans-Medium.woff2` | CC0-1.0 | [NoobSans-CC0.txt](NoobSans-CC0.txt) |
| `NotoSansSC-VF.subset.woff2` | OFL-1.1 | [NotoSansSC-OFL.txt](NotoSansSC-OFL.txt) |

NoobSans output fonts are full, unmodified files. [Replacement evidence](../../../docs/provenance/fonts/replacement-fonts.json) records their reviewed sources, hashes and sizes. The other active families retain their adjacent SIL Open Font License notices. Keep those notices when redistributing the fonts.

[Historical font evidence](../../../docs/provenance/historical-font-licenses/index.json) preserves replaced families' notices and original paths outside the runtime font directories. Those replaced binaries are not bundled in this revision.
